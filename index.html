Fact 4:Wombat poop

Wombats are the only known animals in the world that produce cube-shaped poop. This unusual shape happens because of how their digestive tract works, not because they have a square anus. A wombat’s intestines have alternating stiff and flexible sections that squeeze and dry out waste as it moves along, and the final two meters of the intestine apply uneven pressure that forms flat edges and sharp corners. Because wombats live in dry environments, their bodies absorb almost all the moisture from their food, making the droppings very firm and dry so they hold their boxy shape. Wombats produce around 80 to 100 of these small cubes every night and stack them near their burrows, logs, and rocks to mark their territory and communicate with other wombats. The flat sides keep the poop from rolling away, allowing their scent markers to stay right where they leave them.
